Privacy Policy

Effective: 22 October 2026

Kıble asks for no account and contains no ads, analytics or tracking. Your prayer records and settings stay on your phone. Data leaves the device only to make a feature work, in the cases listed one by one below.

What we don't collect

  • No account, username, email address or password.
  • No identity details such as date of birth, gender or phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking software.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is never read and you are never shown ads.
  • Your prayer records, location and settings are not sent to us.
  • Your personal data is never sold or transferred to anyone.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

  • Your settings: language, theme, calculation method, school, reminder and sound choices.
  • The cities you choose and your last location (to calculate prayer times and the qibla).
  • Your prayer records: prayed marks, streak and make-up (qada) tracking.
  • Your favourite mosque and the last nearby mosque search results.
  • What your widgets show. This is shared on the phone between the app and its widgets.

We don't see, back up or restore these records.

Backups

Export in Settings creates a backup file. It contains your prayer and qada records, chosen cities and preferences. Your last GPS location, mosque search results and purchase information are not included. You choose where the file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, a messaging app and so on). That service stores it under its own terms; we never receive a copy.

Your location

Prayer times and the qibla direction are calculated on your phone; your location is not sent to us for this. If you don't grant location access you can choose a city by hand and the app keeps working. Your location is sent only to the search services listed in the table below, to run that search.

When data leaves your device

To be transparent: data leaves your device in the cases below. Each one serves a feature, and none includes your name, email or any account details. Like any internet service, these services can see the IP address of the connection.

The Dynamic Island card and the end service

So that the prayer card can close by itself after the prayer time begins, we use a small server service. It is the only server-side part of the app and is used only if you turn the card on and agree.

  • What is sent: the card's push token (a code Apple creates only for that card; it contains neither your name nor a device identifier), the time the card should end and the “Prayer time has begun” text shown on it (in the app's language). Location, city and prayer records are not sent.
  • How long it is kept: this information waits in a Google Cloud queue until the end task runs. A card can be set up at most 72 hours ahead; if the end message can't be delivered it is retried for 15 more minutes. When the task finishes it is removed from the queue. The service does not store this information anywhere else.
  • Server logs: Google Cloud access logs may contain IP addresses and connection details. These logs follow Google's standard retention periods.
  • Turning it off: switch off Dynamic Island in Settings; nothing new is sent.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer notifications and alarms are scheduled on your phone, using its own clock; no server is involved. The only message that comes from a server is the end message that closes the card above. It is not shown as a notification.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK you have the right to access your data and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. We keep no record that can be linked to you: the data on your phone is deleted with the app, and the token in the end service leaves the queue when the card closes.
