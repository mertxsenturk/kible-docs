Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 August 2026

Kıble collects nothing about you. There is no account to create, no server of ours, and your data never leaves your phone.

What we do not collect

The app has no backend. This is not a policy choice but the architecture itself: there is nowhere for your data to be sent.

  • No account, username, email address or password is requested.
  • No date of birth, gender, phone number or other identifying details.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is never read; you are shown no ads.
  • Nothing about you is ever sold or shared.

What stays on your phone

The following is stored only in your device’s own storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

  • Your settings: language, theme, calculation method, madhab, alert sound.
  • Your selected city and last known location, used to compute times.
  • Your prayer records: completion marks, streak and missed-prayer tracking.
  • Your alarm configuration.

None of this ever reaches us. We cannot see it, we do not back it up, and we cannot restore it.

Your location

Prayer times and the qibla direction depend on location. Your location is used on your device and is not sent to us. If you decline the location permission, the app asks you to pick a city manually and keeps working.

The three cases where data leaves your device

For full transparency: in the three cases below, data does leave your phone. Each serves a specific function, and none of them carries your identity.

When Where What is sent
You open the “Nearby” tab OpenStreetMap Overpass service (overpass-api.de) Your location and a search radius, to find nearby mosques. No identifying information is included.
You search for a city, or your current place name is shown Apple’s geocoding service The city name you typed, or a coordinate. Governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.
You subscribe Apple / the App Store Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details; we only learn whether a subscription is active.

If you never open the “Nearby” tab, no request is ever made to the Overpass service.

Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled locally on your phone against its own clock. We send no push notifications, and we have no server capable of doing so.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

GDPR and Turkish KVKK rights apply where an organisation holds personal data about you. We hold none, so there is no record to access, correct or erase. To remove your data, delete the app.

Changes

If this document changes, the effective date above is updated. Should an update ever alter what the app collects, we will say so plainly here.
