What the app does

Kıble calculates prayer times, shows the qibla direction, schedules prayer reminders, keeps a prayer log and explains the steps of wudu. Times are computed on your device using the calculation method and madhab you select.

Free and paid

Prayer times and the qibla direction are always free. Widgets, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island countdown, prayer notifications and alarms, missed-prayer tracking, adhan and custom alert sounds, the wudu guide and multiple saved cities are part of the Kıble Plus subscription. A how-to-pray guide and supplications will be added once their texts have been reviewed by a qualified scholar, and will be part of the subscription.

About the religious content

The religious texts in the app were transcribed from their sources and cross-checked against a second source; none were written from memory or paraphrased. Even so, the app is not a religious authority. Prayer times are astronomical calculations and may differ by a few minutes from the official times in your locality depending on method, madhab and elevation. When in doubt, consult your local religious authority.

The qibla direction relies on your phone’s compass, which is affected by metal, magnets and nearby electronics. The app warns you when calibration appears necessary.
